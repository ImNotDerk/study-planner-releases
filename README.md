# Study Planner — downloads

A study planner for one person: subjects, topics and chapters, a timeline and
calendar, progress, search, files, exams and marks. It runs on your own Mac, with
no account and no cloud.

## Download

**[Get the latest version →](https://github.com/ImNotDerk/study-planner-releases/releases/latest)**

On that page, download the file named **`Study-Planner-<version>.zip`** (about
97 MB). Ignore the two *Source code* links — they are GitHub's own, and contain
none of this app.

1. Unzip it and drag the **Study Planner** folder to your Applications folder (or
   your home folder).
2. Open **Study Planner.app** — it is the first item in that folder.
3. **The first time, macOS will refuse to open it.** The message says "Apple could
   not verify Study Planner is free of malware": click **Done**, then open
   **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down to *Security*, and click
   **Open Anyway** next to the message about Study Planner. Enter your password or
   use Touch ID, then click *Open*.

   That is once per copy. The app is not signed with a paid Apple developer
   certificate, which is the only reason macOS asks.

Your plan is kept in `~/Library/Application Support/Study Planner`, so updating or
replacing the app never touches it. Use **Back up Study Planner.app** to copy it
into a zip you can keep in iCloud Drive.

## Updating

Once installed, Study Planner updates itself: a dot appears beside **About &
updates** in the left-hand rail when a newer version is published, and one button
installs it and restarts the app. You never need this page again unless you are
putting it on another Mac.
